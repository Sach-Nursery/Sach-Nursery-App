Quick Test Guide for the Updated Features 1. Unified Inventory & Sales: - Product cards show Hindi Name larger on top and English Name smaller underneath. - Items with stock < threshold (e.g., काला मखमली गुलाब, कमल का पौधा, हापुस आम, लैवेंडर, मॉन्स्टेरा) appear highlighted in red with a Low Stock badge. - Click the (i) Info icon next to Add to Cart to open dedicated Hindi plant care instructions. - Click Share Price-Free Catalog PDF to enter a WhatsApp number, generate a price-free A4 PDF of only in-stock items (dates formatted DD-MM-YYYY), and open WhatsApp. - Scan or click the Digital Catalog QR widget displayed directly in the interface. 2. POS Checkout & WhatsApp: - Customer Name and Mobile Number are optional during checkout. - Completing a sale generates a strictly English invoice paired with strictly Hindi plant care instructions for the purchased plants. 3. Stock Management & Adjustments: - Stock Adjustment is hidden from the main menu and accessed inside Stock Management. - Includes a searchable plant dropdown, Damaged Stock (खराब सामान), and Giveaway (मुफ्त वितरण) with automatic Hindi reason translation and password lock. 4. Summary Exports & Role-Based Access: - Sale Summary and Purchase Summary are removed from the main menu and accessed from inside Inventory & Sales and Purchases respectively, each with its own independent Excel export button. - Log in with Staff Counter / staff@123 (or create a Staff user in Settings) to verify restricted access to Inventory & Sales + Sale Summary only, along with the administrator-configurable Staff edit time window in Settings.
