Implemented Requirements Summary 1. Theme & Interface: The Dark/Light theme toggle exists exclusively inside the Settings page (SettingsPage). Typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans + Noto Sans Devanagari for crisp English and Hindi readability. 2. Navigation & Dashboard: The sidebar starts collapsed (sidebarExpanded = false) with persistent Menu toggle and persistent Workers & Attendance shortcut in the top bar. Low Stock Alerts and Recent Sales have been omitted from the Dashboard, replaced by the prominent Scan QR Code button, Inventory & Catalog shortcut card, Sale Summary card, and Purchase Summary card. 3. QR Scanner Integration: Full-screen scanner supports live camera QR scanning (html5-qrcode) plus instant lookup across all preloaded and user-uploaded items. Scanned cards display only uploaded photo (if present), product name, and category—selling rate and stock quantities are strictly hidden. Checking the card checkbox reveals planting season, care, water, sunlight, and fertilizer details. 4. Inventory & Catalog: 12-card grid layout per batch with IntersectionObserver infinite scrolling, bold category headers, dynamic quantity typing, Add to Cart, and smart English/Hindi/Hinglish search (including phonetic Devanagari transliteration). 5. Sales & Purchases: Grid layouts for both; Purchases increase stock and POS sales decrease stock automatically. Central POS checkout supports Cash and UPI only with automated split-payment calculations and total bill discounts. No edit controls appear on the Add to Cart cards. 6. Stock Management: Dedicated page showing photo, name, stock, threshold, and selling price, plus Stock Adjustments (Donations / Damage) where entries lock automatically and require PIN/password verification (msg@9999) to unlock and edit. 7. Summary Reports: Both Sale Summary and Purchase Summary display detailed bill-by-bill histories when a single day is selected, and aggregated item/category totals across 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, All Time, or Custom date ranges. 8. Worker & Attendance: Persistent top-bar shortcut button leading to worker directory, daily roll call, and monthly wage calculation. 9. Databases & Security: Preloaded multilingual scientific Plant Care Database that auto-queries GBIF/Wikimedia when adding new entries, password-protected editing, and password-protected Bill Gallery photo deletion. 10. Authentication: Masked password login (Davinder Singh / msg@9999).
